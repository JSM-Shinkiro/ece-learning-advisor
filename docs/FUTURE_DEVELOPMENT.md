# Future development notes

Team P07 · November 15, 2026

These proposals are for later releases of ECE Learning Advisor. [Current app scope](APP_SUMMARY.md) · [Final submission readiness](FINAL_READINESS.md)

## Pima Community College transfer tracks

Pilot a Pima Community College to UArizona Electrical and Computer Engineering pathway. Let students select their target transfer term and degree option, enter completed or planned Pima courses, and see verified UArizona equivalents, applicable degree requirements, and remaining preparation. Cover the EE and CE degree options as their transfer plans are verified.

Keep course equivalency, applicability to the selected major, and transfer admission as distinct fields. Attach an institution, catalog year, source, and any published credit or grade conditions to each mapping. Flag indirect credit and uncertain matches for advising. Check one complete example against the applicable Pima pathway, AZTransfer equivalencies, and UArizona advised plan. [Pima transfer partnerships](https://www.pima.edu/academics-programs/transfer-degrees-partners/partnering-institutions.html) · [AZTransfer tools](https://www.arizonatransfer.org/tools/) · [UArizona Engineering transfer pathways](https://www.engr.arizona.edu/undergrad-admissions/transfer)

## Broader job and career search

Expand the employer search with official career feeds and public employer boards. Start with Tucson and Phoenix employers across the current career paths. Add internships, co-ops, and entry-level roles, with filters for location, work arrangement, skills, and specialization. Deduplicate shared postings and show the source, last check, and listing status.

Add career profiles for students who are exploring a field before applying. Describe typical work, common skills, entry requirements, and paths into a role. Link the profile to UArizona courses, relevant practice projects, and dated occupational data. [Current opportunity workflow](APP_SUMMARY.md#other-app-functions) · [BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/)

## Course sections and instructor ratings

Show the published section and each assigned instructor when a student chooses a course and term. Start with links to a verified Rate My Professors instructor profile or the provider's search page. Match the instructor and institution before attaching a profile. Label unassigned instructors and uncertain matches where they occur. Link to UArizona's Student Course Survey instructions for students with a NetID. [UArizona student survey access](https://ucatt.arizona.edu/assessment/student-course-surveys/scs-students)

Displaying numeric Rate My Professors ratings requires a permitted data source under its terms. If available, show the source, rating type, review count, and date checked beside the instructor. Identify an instructor-wide rating or a course-specific rating so students know what was reviewed. [Rate My Professors terms](https://www.ratemyprofessors.com/terms-of-use)

## Other UArizona departments

Pilot advising for a complete degree option in one adjacent department, such as Computer Science, Optical Sciences, or Systems and Industrial Engineering. Give each added program a browsable course inventory, a reviewed set of career connections, and its own catalog-year requirements. Use existing cross-department elective links to connect related paths. Verify prerequisites, enrollment restrictions, and degree applicability for the chosen program. [UArizona program catalog](https://catalog.arizona.edu/programs)

## Additional ideas

| Idea | First usable version |
| --- | --- |
| **Term and section planner** | Place planned courses in a term-by-term roadmap. Show credit loads, known prerequisite conflicts, section availability, and time overlaps. Mark future terms as estimates when schedules are unavailable. |
| **Minor and certificate overlap** | Extend the Mathematics overlap view to Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, and relevant certificates. Show shared courses and remaining requirements using the applicable catalog year and double-counting rules. |
| **Side-by-side plan comparison** | Compare two saved scenarios, such as an EE route and a CE route. Show shared courses, remaining credits, and assumptions to check. Build on the Advisor's JSON profile export and restore. |
| **Portfolio and research opportunities** | Connect skills on a career roadmap to a small set of verified projects, undergraduate labs, and student competitions. Give each project a concrete deliverable the student can show. |
| **Report a catalog issue** | Let a student flag an outdated link, prerequisite, or transfer match from its source card. Queue the affected record, catalog year, and supporting link for maintainer review. |
| **Recorded neural walkthrough** | Let students replay a real embedding comparison for a fictional student during preview lessons. Show the recorded query, model, capture date, and corpus revision so the example can be reproduced. |

Pima transfer is the first suggested pilot because it adds a new starting point and can be tested against a single term and degree option. A section planner provides the course, term, and instructor matching needed for instructor links. The job search can expand one career area at a time as employer sources are verified.
