Technical Electives II (R15492/L20) lists 300- and 400-level coursework, excluding 390s and 490s. The advised plan directs students in both degree options to consult their major advisor for course approval. The public guidance lists a category, so the two CSC courses appear as candidates. Their placement in an individual degree plan and section enrollment need separate confirmation. ## Advisor behavior

- A selected path adds its five records to the 34-chunk core for that request. While **Still exploring** is selected, a single matching direction can guide the answer to a wireless DSP or feedback-control question. The learner can explicitly save that direction.
- The roadmap uses ECE courses and the matching practice resource. The path panel shows the outside-ECE candidate, the reason it fits, and the official elective guidance. A question naming the candidate can also bring its course card into the plan. As elsewhere, completed and currently taking courses are excluded from suggestions.
- Both directions work in **Courses & Resources** and with the **Opportunities** title filters. The position examples illustrate possible work. The configured boards determine which listings appear during a search.
- The combined collection has 44 evidence chunks. The 34-chunk core and the ten-chunk baseline retain their archived evaluation identities. The two new practice resources use dated links and suggested exercises.
